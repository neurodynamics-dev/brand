Section label for the formal register; use instead of mono uppercase eyebrows.
```jsx
<Kicker variant="rule" label="Resultados" index="ii"/>
<Kicker variant="folio" label="Métodos e população" index="2"/>
<Kicker variant="tint" label="Sumário executivo" family="sky"/>
```
- rule: hairline above, sentence case label, optional roman numeral.
- folio: large Instrument Serif numeral beside a short label.
- tint: light family panel with dark family serif italic.
