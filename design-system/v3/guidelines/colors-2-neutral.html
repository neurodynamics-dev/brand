Named after the brain's own tissues: Sulco (the deep folds of the cortex) for deep grounds and ink, Pia (pia mater) for cool greys, Medula (white matter) for warm paper. Plain only. Paper is white, Medula light or Pia light; text is Sulco medium; rules are Pia medium.
